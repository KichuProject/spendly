FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Spendly v3.0.0? Here is everything you need to know.

What is new in Spendly v3.0.0 compared to v1.0 and v2.0?
Version 1.0 introduced basic manual ledger tracking. Version 2.0 brought group bill splitting and PDF exports. Version 3.0.0 introduces our headline Multilingual Voice Assistant (English, Tamil, and Mixed speech), multi-item AI extraction, 1-tap interactive confirmation popups, Apple OLED & luxury light design system, and unified web/iOS hosting!
How does the Voice Assistant parse Tamil and Mixed speech?
Spendly utilizes custom natural language parsing tuned for regional Indian speech patterns. Phrases like "Tea 20 rs and auto 150 rupees" or Tamil speech are parsed automatically into separate categories (Food & Transport) with exact numeric values.
How do I run Spendly on Web and iOS?
Click "Continue on Web / iOS" anywhere on the landing page. It opens the web application directly in your browser or lets iOS Safari users add it to their Home Screen as a full-screen PWA app with zero installation required!
Is my financial data private and secure?
Yes! All transactions and voice inputs are encrypted end-to-end. Your financial data is securely linked to your account with zero third-party data tracking or selling.
How does group expense splitting & settlement work?
Add friends to a shared group expense, specify who paid, and Spendly automatically calculates net balances. Members can settle debts with 1-tap via UPI payment apps!
Can I export my monthly spending reports as PDF or CSV?
Yes! From the Analytics dashboard, tap "Export Report" to generate clean, professionally formatted PDF or CSV monthly summary statements for offline bookkeeping.
